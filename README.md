# SpeakTab

**The latest public release is 10.14.70. The new note-entry release is being revised for clearer beginner instructions and has not been published.**

SpeakTab is an accessible Windows tab and score reader for keyboard, screen-reader and Braille users. Open supported music, choose a supplied part, read notes and lyrics, hear local practice previews, slow down, loop passages and retain practice notes. Optional connected AI is separate from ordinary local reading.

## Read and practise

Ctrl+O opens music, F2 finds supported sources, and Ctrl+T chooses a supplied MusicXML or MuseScore part. Ctrl+2 focuses the score. Left/Right follows musical time; Up/Down inspects simultaneous notes, strings or lyrics. F3 opens complete details. Ctrl+Shift+I opens supplied score information. Ctrl+P plays and Ctrl+Shift+P stops. Practice contains loops, bookmarks and personal notes. Ctrl+S saves a reader arrangement; File > My readings resumes it. Companion sheets and exported reading copies remain separate from original music.

## Note entry is being revised before release

The local beta adds a separate composer for piano, six-string guitar, Voice and their combinations, with reviewed note entry, selectors, projects and MusicXML export. The local version 74 has 29 tutorials. These features and their manuals are not yet included in the public version 70 download.

The next revision is improving explanations for people who are new to reading or writing music: what each control does, what to enter, the expected result, how to correct a mistake and how to leave a dialog. It will also add a direct New score shortcut. Note entry will remain clearly labelled beta.

## Optional connections and accuracy

F4 connects written ChatGPT, F5 focuses its question, Enter sends and F6 reads the answer. Ctrl+Alt+L controls the separate free reader-command microphone. F8 starts the configured live teacher and F9 stops it. F12 silences enabled app speech without stopping music or microphones. Connected services may incur charges.

PDF/OCR and AI recovery need comparison with the source. Importable output does not guarantee complete or correct music. Playback and expressive effects are practice approximations; vocal previews are wordless. Physical screen-reader, Braille, microphone, audio and live-model quality require hands-on review. Packages exclude private music, readings, profiles, credentials and diagnostic dumps. Previous public releases remain available.
