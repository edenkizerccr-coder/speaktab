# SpeakTab

SpeakTab 10.14.76 is an accessible Windows tab and score reader with **beta note entry** for piano, six-string guitar and vocal parts. Use a keyboard, screen reader or Braille display to read supported music, inspect notes and lyrics, hear local practice previews, loop passages and retain practice notes.

## Learn the musical workflow

The full manual and all 29 guided tasks explain SpeakTab for musicians with ordinary computer skills and no previous notation-software experience. They identify the controls, explain their musical choices, describe what appears after an action and give ways to correct the result. The manual retains the complete menu reference, advanced information and observed recovery limitations.

Guided tutorials shows one instruction at a time. Next step moves inside a task; Next lesson changes subjects. Show whole lesson displays the complete reference. Optional Read displayed text uses local Windows voice. F1 provides relevant control help, and the composer’s How to compose opens its complete reference.

## Read and practise

Ctrl+O opens music, F2 finds supported sources, and Ctrl+T chooses a supplied part. Ctrl+2 focuses musical positions. Left/Right follows musical time; Up/Down inspects simultaneous notes, strings or lyrics. F3 opens full details. Ctrl+Shift+I reports supplied score information. Ctrl+P plays and Ctrl+Shift+P stops. Practice offers loops, bookmarks and comments. Ctrl+S saves a reader arrangement; My readings resumes it. Original music, companion sheets and exported reports remain distinct.

## Write scores with beta note entry

Ctrl+Shift+N opens the separate composer. Ctrl+E opens note selection. Choose the part, named staff, starting position, pitch and duration. Enter adds a note, chord or rest and advances by its duration, crossing into the next measure automatically. Tab moves between controls. A position message reports where the next note begins. Uncheck Advance to keep the position. Add this pitch to chord collects simultaneous tones without advancing; Enter records the complete chord.

Piano choices name Upper and Lower staff. Rhythm line 1 is normal entry, including on both piano staves. Additional options are hidden until requested; Use a tuplet is off for ordinary notes. Chord symbol is an optional written label; choose actual played tones separately. Supported full phrases and shorthand remain available, with a named measure continuing within a description until changed.

Review several measures together, read or hear their exact proposed changes, then Apply. Edit existing events to replace music and use Undo/Redo for applied changes. Save project keeps applied music and unfinished descriptions, selected notes, choices, positions and pending review; Open project restores them. Export MusicXML writes an XML score of applied music, with a warning when unfinished work would be omitted. Examples throughout the manual and tutorials are optional suggestions.

Note entry remains beta. Inspect every intended pitch, duration, staff, rhythm line and lyric before sharing. Multiple parts, lyrics, ties, supported expression, guitar patterns and measure operations are available. Authoring does not provide engraving, arbitrary imported-score editing, percussion, editable grace notes or advanced guitar techniques.

## Optional connections and accuracy

F4 connects written ChatGPT, F5 focuses its question, Enter sends and F6 reads the answer. Ctrl+Alt+L controls the separate free reader-command microphone. F8 starts the configured connected teacher and F9 stops it. F12 silences app speech without changing microphone state. Connected services may incur charges. Current-file/another-file AI remediation can start automatically with a saved connection; the separate ChatGPT-window handoff requires your paste, attachment and send in the browser.

PDF recognition and AI recovery can omit or misread music. Read coverage, compare the original and retain warnings with provisional output. Importable does not mean complete or correct. Playback effects are practice approximations, and vocal previews are wordless. Physical screen-reader, Braille, microphone, audio and live-model quality need hands-on review. Public packages exclude private music, readings, settings, profiles, credentials and diagnostic dumps. Previous public releases remain available.
